BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful
answers.

How is a project priced?+

By scope: deliverables, production requirements, locations, formats, timing and revision rounds. Your proposal sets these out before work begins.

Can we start with one shoot?+

Yes. You can commission a focused production without committing to an ongoing partnership.

Do you handle posting and analytics?+

These can be part of a social partnership. They are not automatically included with a film or content production booking.

What do you need from us?+

Your goal, audience, timing and any existing brand assets. If you have not worked out the deliverables yet, start with the goal and we’ll help define them.
