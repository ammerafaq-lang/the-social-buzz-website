BEFORE WE START

Useful answers.
No sales fog.

How much does a project cost?+

It depends on the goal, production requirements, number of deliverables and timeline. Share the brief and an indicative budget range; we will recommend a focused scope instead of padding the proposal.

Can we start with one project?+

Yes. A focused production or campaign is often the best way to establish the creative direction before moving into an ongoing partnership.

Do you only handle production?+

No. Projects can include strategy, creative direction, scripting, production, post-production and social support. The scope will state exactly what is included.

What happens after I enquire?+

We review the goal, timeline and requirements, then respond with the most useful next step: a short call, a request for missing details, or a proposed direction and scope.
